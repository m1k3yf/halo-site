Halo

Privacy policy

Effective October 15, 2026

Halo is a desktop email application for macOS. This policy explains what information Halo accesses, how it is used, and where it is stored.

Information Halo accesses

When you connect a Google account, Halo asks for permission to:

  • Read, organize, compose and send your Gmail messages (the gmail.modify permission), so Halo can show your inbox, archive, label and trash messages, save drafts, and send email on your behalf when you ask it to.
  • See your email address (the userinfo.email permission), so Halo knows which account you connected.

Halo uses this information only to provide its email features to you inside the app.

Where your information is stored

  • Your email is stored only on your own Mac, in Halo's local database.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in your Mac's Keychain.
  • Halo has no servers. Your email and account information are not sent to the developer or to any third party. Halo communicates only with Google's servers, to sync your email.

What Halo does not do

  • Halo does not sell, rent or share your information.
  • Halo does not use your information for advertising.
  • Halo does not use your information to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • No person reads your email through Halo. Messages are processed only on your Mac to display them to you.

Google API Services

Halo's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your information

Disconnecting an account in Halo's Settings removes that account's email and sign-in token from your Mac. You can also revoke Halo's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting Halo removes all of its local data.

Changes

If Halo's handling of information changes, for example if optional features that send data to another service are added, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and such features will be off unless you turn them on.
