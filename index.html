Halo

Every inbox. One place.

Halo is a keyboard-first email app for Mac. It brings all of your email accounts into one unified inbox, so you can read, reply and get to inbox zero without reaching for the mouse.

Private by design

Halo runs on your Mac. Your email is stored on your own computer, sign-in tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain, and Halo has no servers of its own. Read the privacy policy for details.

Status

Halo is a personal project in active development and is not publicly available.
